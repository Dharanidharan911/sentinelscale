# SentinelScale — Traffic Intelligence Heuristic Baseline Benchmark Report

## 1. Overview
This benchmark report quantitatively measures the performance, accuracy, and operational characteristics of the **current heuristic Traffic Intelligence implementation (`traffic-rules-v1`)** prior to the introduction of machine learning models (such as Isolation Forest).

> **Important Disclosure**: The evaluation dataset utilized in this benchmark is **synthetic and scenario-derived**, generated under controlled parameter distributions across canonical traffic conditions. These results do not claim production-level ML accuracy; they establish an objective, reproducible baseline to answer: *"Did subsequent ML models actually improve SentinelScale's traffic intelligence?"*

---

## 5. Limitations & Caveats

1. **Synthetic Scenario Bias**: The synthetic dataset generator samples from clean parameter intervals. Real-world edge traffic features complex multimodal distributions, low-and-slow botnets that emulate legitimate browser headers, and distributed residential proxy swarms that evade simple single-IP concentration.
2. **Unmeasured Features**: Features such as sub-second inter-arrival variance, JA3/JA4 TLS fingerprints, and full IP Shannon entropy are not currently ingested by the gateway layer and therefore could not be evaluated.
3. **Threshold Sensitivity**: The heuristic model relies on deterministic hyperparameter thresholds (e.g. `IP_CONCENTRATION_HIGH = 0.40`). Sophisticated adversaries operating just below these thresholds (e.g. at 38% concentration across a rotating proxy pool) may require statistical ML (such as Isolation Forests) or behavioral baselining for detection.

---

## 6. Interpretation & Role for Future Machine Learning
The deterministic heuristic baseline establishes a fast, predictable floor:
- **Strengths**: Extremely low latency ($< 0.03 \text{ ms}$), zero false positives on flash crowds (Scenario B correctly classified as legitimate despite $3-6\times$ RPS surge), and unambiguous explanations in `top_signals`.
- **Target for ML (Phase M1-4 / Isolation Forest)**: The primary role of introducing an unsupervised Isolation Forest or semi-supervised anomaly detector in future phases will be to detect subtle, distributed multi-dimensional anomalies that evade fixed heuristic thresholds without increasing false positive rates on organic flash crowds.
